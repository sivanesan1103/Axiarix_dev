## Steps shown

1. Platform owner creates the organisation with the wizard.
2. Asha accepts the invitation (Mailpit), sets her password and signs in.
3. Asha adds the other five people and makes Kavya the IT department owner.
4. Imran writes a checklist with two questions and sends it to Asha for review; Asha publishes it.
5. Imran creates the Q3 assessment for Main Branch / IT and releases it to Kavya.
6. Kavya gives question 1 to Ravi and answers question 2 herself.
7. Ravi answers question 1 and uploads evidence.
8. Kavya submits for audit.
9. Meera reviews every answer and file and raises a finding.
10. Ravi plans and completes the corrective action; Meera verifies it and closes the finding.
11. Imran approves and the final PDF report is issued.
12. Dr. Rao views the dashboard and the report.
